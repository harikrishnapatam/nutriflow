💡 Pro Tips

Nutrition Habits That Stick

Small consistent changes beat drastic overhauls every time.

01
💧

Hydrate First

Drink at least 500ml of water upon waking. Dehydration is often mistaken for hunger, leading to unnecessary snacking.

02
🎨

Eat the Rainbow

Aim for 5+ different colored fruits and vegetables daily. Each color represents unique phytonutrients and antioxidants.

03
🍽️

Mindful Eating

Eat slowly and without screens. It takes 20 minutes for your brain to register fullness – slow down and savor every bite.

04
🧩

Prep Ahead

Spend 1–2 hours on weekends batch cooking grains, proteins, and veggies. Healthy eating is easy when the food is ready.

05
🕕

Time Your Meals

Eat protein-rich meals post-workout within 2 hours to maximize muscle protein synthesis and recovery.

06
📖

Read Labels

Scan ingredient lists. If the first 3 ingredients are sugars or you can't pronounce them, put it back on the shelf.
